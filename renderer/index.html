The Forge

This app watches your screen

Read this before you start. It is short and it is all of it.

What leaves your Mac

  • The titles of your open windows, including browser tab titles, about every 10 seconds. Not page contents, not URLs, not keystrokes.
  • The names of applications you are running.
  • Whether this app is open, and when it was last heard from.
  • Your name and email, so organisers know whose machine this is.

What stays on your Mac

  • A rolling recording of your screen. Only the last minute is kept — everything older is thrown away continuously.
  • It is never uploaded. If an organiser flags something, that minute is saved to a folder on this machine and they come and look at it. A single still is sent so they know whether it is worth the walk.

Other things you should know

  • macOS will show a screen-recording icon in your menu bar the whole time. That is this app, and it is expected.
  • Closing this app is against the hackathon rules. Nothing here punishes you for it — an organiser sees it and decides.
  • Window titles are deleted at the end of the event.
  • How to quit and uninstall afterwards is in the README that came with the app, and on the last screen here.

Two macOS permissions

macOS will not let the agent do its job until you grant these.

Screen & System Audio Recording
Required. Without it the agent records a blank screen and nobody finds out until an organiser opens the clip.
Accessibility
Needed to read window titles. Skip it and the agent reports running applications only — organisers will see that it is switched off.

macOS does not apply a new screen-recording grant to an app that is already running. Once you have ticked it, the agent has to restart.
